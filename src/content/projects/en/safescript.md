It reached the finals of the TEKNOFEST 2024 Technology for the Benefit of Humanity competition in the Health and First Aid category, where we presented it to the jury and to visitors at our booth. I developed the frontend: I wrote the screens the physician uses and connected them to the API written by my teammate."
